## Question

Who may record acceptance verification of a feature (implemented → verified)?

## Why it matters

REQ-002 criterion 3 and plan I-4 require "separately evidenced acceptance verification" but do not say whose evidence counts. Today nothing sets verified automatically.

## Options

- **A**: Only a human in the MarkView UI (an Accept action with a note); AI never sets verified.
- **B**: A human, or AI when it supplies verification evidence and every acceptance criterion of the active requirements is ticked.

## Recommended

A

## Answer

B. A human, or AI when it supplies verification evidence and every acceptance criterion of the active requirements is ticked. (Boris, 2026-11-05, in the goal session: "Человек или AI с доказательствами".)
