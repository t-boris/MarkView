## Question

What is a confirmed regression, and who may reopen the original issue for it?

## Why it matters

Q-003 chose reopening the original issue, REQ-002 criterion 5 says "a confirmed regression reopens it", but no record defines what confirms a regression or who may do it.

## Options

- **A**: A human in the UI (with a reason), or AI when a linked bug report names the original in regression_of and is not closed as invalid or duplicate.
- **B**: Only a human in the UI; AI may only create the linked bug.
- **C**: As A, and also automatically when a required check fails on the default branch for the completed work.

## Recommended

A

## Answer

A. A human in the UI (with a reason), or AI when a linked bug report names the original in regression_of and is not closed as invalid or duplicate. A failing check alone only shows as a discrepancy. (Boris, 2026-11-05: "Человек в UI или AI по связанному багу".) Destinations chosen by the implementation: feature → implementing, bug → open.
