## Question

What does "verified completion" mean for automatic closure of a GitHub issue?

## Why it matters

REQ-006 and Q-006 close a GitHub issue only on verified completion of every linked work; the current manual Sync also counts archived-without-implementation and duplicate bugs as done, which REQ-006 criterion 4 forbids.

## Options

- **A**: Implementation completed through evidence (merge, checks, required release): feature implemented or verified, bug fixed or closed after a fix. Overrides, archived without implementation, duplicate or invalid bugs and unknown status do not qualify.
- **B**: Only acceptance: every linked feature verified and every bug closed after a fix.

## Recommended

A

## Answer

A. Implementation completed through evidence (merge, checks, required release) is verified completion: feature implemented or verified, bug fixed or closed after a fix. Overrides, archived without implementation, duplicate or invalid bugs and unknown status do not qualify. (Boris, 2026-11-05: "Реализация с доказательствами".)
