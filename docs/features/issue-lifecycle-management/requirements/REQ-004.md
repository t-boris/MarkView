## Statement

The system shall make lifecycle decisions and synchronization outcomes explainable through an inspectable history.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Each applied transition identifies the previous and resulting status, actor, time, reason and supporting evidence.
- [ ] Human overrides are visibly identified together with their reason and the conditions they bypassed.
- [ ] The person can inspect blocked transitions, missing evidence and discrepancies between local status and observed work.
- [ ] GitHub synchronization results distinguish confirmed success, failure and an unconfirmed outcome.
