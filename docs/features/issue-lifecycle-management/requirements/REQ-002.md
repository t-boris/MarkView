## Statement

Apply agreed lifecycle rules consistently to manual and automated status changes.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] When a supported work event satisfies an approved transition rule, the corresponding issue status is updated automatically.
- [ ] An attempted transition that does not meet the approved rule produces an understandable result without silently reporting success.
- [ ] Feature and bug transitions follow their respective approved lifecycle rules.
