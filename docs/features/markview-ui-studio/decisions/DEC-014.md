## Context

The Screens menu (found by "Discover Application Screens") lists screens with their prerequisites, but the list is cleared at every launch and choosing a screen runs a slow assistant request (median about 28 s) that is verified on the one visible page. Boris wants the main screens selectable and already filled, with the assistant deciding how to fill them. There is a single WKWebView and one simulator, so a screen cannot be verified in the background without disturbing the page the person is using.

## Alternatives

1. Save only the list; prepare each screen at its first click (cheaper, nothing is filled ahead)
2. Prepare and verify every screen ahead on the visible page (disturbs the person, slow, expensive)
3. Prepare every discovered screen ahead (the longest and most expensive, many assistant timeouts)

## Decision

The screen list is team-owned and saved in .markview/studio/screens.json with, per screen, its scenario id, status, preparation and verification dates and the hashes of the files its source names. It is found automatically once, after the environment map. At most 8 main screens are prepared ahead, one at a time: only the assistant phase and the static validator run (no page, no requirement tracking), always derived from the built-in Populated preset, never chained, and the result is saved as an ordinary scenario (id screen-<name>). Choosing a ready screen activates its scenario at once; its first activation verifies the destination through the existing restore and a miss falls back to the current correction request. A change of the source files marks the screen as needing refresh. Other screens are prepared at their first click and saved too.

## Reason

It gives what Boris asked for (main screens ready, filled by the assistant) without a second page and without a verification that disturbs the person. The cost is bounded (8 screens, one at a time, about 4 to 8 minutes and $2 on Claude) and paid once per source revision, not at every click.

## Consequences

A prepared screen is only "prepared" until opened once, then "verified": it may still need one correction round at first open. "Filled" means plausible simulated data. The scenario files are committed with the project, so their data is kept small. The change() request is split: the assistant phase moves into a function the preparer and change() share.
