## Context

The cycle-time log is a second observer of status changes next to the workflow journal. It diffs statuses on every reload with a hard-coded vocabulary, cannot tell work from a flow migration, a baseline or an unverified outside edit, and is per Mac and immutable. The Kanban switch of 2026-11-09 12:52:59 wrote 33 false events and the log cannot be corrected.

## Alternatives

1. B: keep the log and patch its rules (ignore replacements, change the aggregation): cheaper, but keeps two logs and the hard-coded vocabulary

## Decision

Cycle time is computed read-only from .dde/lifecycle/transitions.jsonl and the project workflow (statuses with roles, process variants). Records with cause replace and baseline are not events; external records are shown with a data-quality mark. The observer (observeLifecycle, recordSpecReadyIfMissing), the fixed LifecycleStage chain as the source of steps, and the per-Mac stage log as the source of status-derived numbers are removed. GitHub/git evidence capture and the model probe stay as the evidence layer. Supersedes DEC-001 (storage of the stage log), DEC-002 (hybrid capture), DEC-003/DEC-006 (fixed stage list) and DEC-008 (automatic triggers) of lifecycle-event-log-cycle-time-analytics.

## Reason

One record of state changes, workflow-aware, with a cause on every record; a rule fix repairs history because everything is recomputable. Chosen by Boris on 2026-11-09 (direction A).

## Consequences

Metrics are defined by status roles (not-started, active, completed, cancelled), so they follow the standard, Kanban and custom flows. Numbers of the old stage log are not carried over; the status-derived stages and the 33 artefacts are dropped. The journal exists only since 2026-11-05 for some projects, so older items have no status history unless a backfill is built (spike).
