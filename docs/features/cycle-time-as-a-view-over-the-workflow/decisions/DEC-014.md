## Context

Absolute limits do not fit different projects, flows and processes.

## Alternatives



## Decision

Pace is judged against the project's own finished items of the same metric (and process where it matters): at most P50 green, at most P85 amber, above red; fewer than 8 samples gives grey "not enough history". An item in progress is judged by its age in the current status against finished time in that status; not-started statuses are neutral. Facts (checks failing, reopened, blocked, delivery waiting) and data-quality marks are separate signals and never share the pace colour. A configurable limit per status is not built.

## Reason

No settings to maintain, explainable in one sentence, adapts to each project. Recommended in the review and accepted by Boris.

## Consequences

A new project shows grey until it has history. The trend arrow compares the last 30 days with the 30 before and is coloured only with at least 5 samples in each and a change over 20 %.
