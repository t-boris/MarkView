## Statement

The system shall mark durations with three separate signals: pace (green, amber, red against the project's own percentiles, grey under 8 samples), facts (reopened, a request the rules refused, delivery waiting for acceptance) and data quality (inconsistent, missing boundary, bulk-created, external edit, override, excluded), and shall list the items that need attention first, each with one sentence of reason. Failing checks are not a fact here: they need fresh GitHub state, which the journal does not hold.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A finished item at or under the project's median for its metric is green, up to P85 amber, above P85 red; with fewer than 8 finished items the colour is grey
- [ ] An item in an active status longer than the P85 of finished time in that status is red with the sentence 'in-progress 9 d; usually 1.3 d, 85 % finish in 3 d'
- [ ] Not-started statuses are neutral
- [ ] Data-quality marks use a marker that is not the pace colour and never turn a number red or green
- [ ] The Needs attention list shows red before amber, and is empty with a plain sentence when nothing needs attention
- [ ] Each metric row shows a trend arrow (last 30 days against the 30 before), coloured only with at least 5 samples in each window and a change over 20 %
- [ ] Green is shown quietly (small dot), red and amber prominently
