## Statement

The system shall let the person open any number of the summary to see how it is made, the items behind it, and the timeline of one item with the records that formed the duration highlighted.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Opening a row shows the definition in words, the items used, the items left out and why (missing start, inconsistent, excluded, outside the window) with counts, and a minimum, P50, P85, maximum bar
- [ ] Each left-out reason lists its items
- [x] The items behind a row are sorted by value, each with its pace colour and bar
- [x] Opening an item shows its stays with times, actors and causes, the two records that formed the chosen duration highlighted, and a button that opens the feature or bug
- [x] The detail uses the same numbers as the summary: a test builds both from one fixture and compares them
