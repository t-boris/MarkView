## Question

How should the cockpit describe and handle work that an unrestricted agent detaches from its terminal when MarkView closes?

## Why it matters

DEC-002 and REQ-006 say execution stops while MarkView is closed. DEC-006 permits a full-access terminal, and TerminalChildProcess can stop its process group but cannot guarantee termination of a separately detached process. This is distinct from the already settled advisory action controls.

## Options

- **A**: Stop the agent terminal and its process group on close, disclose that independently detached processes may continue, and require the user to decide before resuming.
- **B**: Require an independently enforced execution environment that also prevents detached work from continuing after MarkView closes.

## Recommended

A

## Answer

Stop the terminal and its process group; warn that separately detached background processes may continue, and ask the person before continuing.
