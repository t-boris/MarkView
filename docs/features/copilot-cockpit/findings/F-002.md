## Finding

Existing QA cleanup retains only the newest five runs per scenario plus runs referenced by filed bugs; users can also delete runs manually. Cockpit results may therefore lose their underlying evidence while the cockpit still promises traceability. The specification does not say whether cockpit-linked QA runs must be retained or whether a preserved summary and an unavailable-artifact state are sufficient.

> Results remain associated with the plan and build under which they were produced.

## Possible interpretations

- Cockpit references protect QA runs from automatic cleanup, with a defined policy for manual deletion.
- QA retention remains unchanged and the cockpit preserves its own result context while indicating when underlying evidence is unavailable.

## Resolution

Пока существует связанный результат cockpit, автоматическая очистка не удаляет QA-запуск. Вручную его можно удалить после предупреждения о связанных результатах; cockpit сохраняет контекст и показывает, что подробные данные удалены. — see DEC-007.
