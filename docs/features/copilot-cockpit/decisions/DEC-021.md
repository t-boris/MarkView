## Context

DEC-002 and REQ-006 require stopping work on app close, while DEC-006 grants agents full access and permits them to create detached processes outside the terminal process group.

## Alternatives

1. Enforce isolation preventing all detached processes

## Decision

Stop the agent terminal and its process group when pausing or closing MarkView. Warn that separately detached background processes may continue and ask the person before continuing an interrupted run.

## Reason

The person chose this explicit limitation and confirmation in answer to Q-007.

## Consequences

The interface must not claim that all agent-created processes are stopped; resumption requires the person's confirmation.
