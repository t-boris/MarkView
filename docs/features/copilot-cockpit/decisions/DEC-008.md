## Context

Existing QA requires approval of the exact execution plan and a separate acknowledgement of the steps that change data in production. Copilot Cockpit also requires explicit approval of its proposed plan; their relationship was undefined.

## Alternatives

1. Require a separate acknowledgement of production QA data-changing actions after cockpit plan approval.
2. Exclude production QA from the first cockpit version.

## Decision

A single cockpit plan approval also satisfies the production QA data-changing-actions acknowledgement when the user is shown the exact list of those actions and explicitly confirms their execution in production. The approval applies only to the specific QA plan presented.

## Reason

This preserves an explicit, informed production acknowledgement while avoiding a second approval step for the same reviewed QA plan.

## Consequences

REQ-002 must require the review to show the exact production QA plan and its data-changing actions, and to obtain explicit confirmation that they will run in production. The approval must be bound to that specific QA plan; a change to the QA plan invalidates it and requires renewed approval. Existing production-step confirmations remain in force.
