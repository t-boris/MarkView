## Context

DEC-018 kept the plan read-only (refinement by voice or text, no direct editing). With 6.17 the plan was a text scroll: a step showed only an agent name, the QA scenario was raw JSON, and nothing could be changed except by asking the Copilot again. A run was a few text lines under the same form, although it may last days. Boris asked on 2026-11-14 for a graphical plan he can read and edit, with a choice of agent and model per step, and for a separate screen showing who answers questions, who watches progress and what happened.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the plan read-only and refine it only through the Copilot (DEC-018)
2. One screen for plan and run, with a larger run section
3. Keep the Mac awake for long runs (not chosen by the person)

## Decision

The Cockpit has two screens and a list of runs. The plan screen shows the plan as steps on a timeline rail; the person can edit, reorder, duplicate, insert and delete steps, edit the success criteria, and choose the agent and model of every step, of the supervisor and of the planner (the planner may propose a model per step). Approval still covers the whole plan value, so every edit invalidates a previous approval and the reasons approval is blocked are shown on the step they belong to. The run screen shows a control room (coordinator, step agent, supervisor, guard), what needs the person, the flow, and a dated timeline with the steps, the questions asked and answered, and the findings. The run records its own timeline. macOS notifications tell the person when a run pauses, a step fails, a limit is reached, the run finishes, or a step shows no sign of life for 20 minutes.

## Reason

A plan the person cannot read or change cannot be approved with confidence, and a run that lasts days is useless to supervise without a record of what happened and a way to be called when it needs them. Direct editing does not weaken the approval: it is bound to the exact edited value, as before.

## Consequences

DEC-018 and the first-version scope line that excludes direct editing no longer hold if this is accepted. A step's model is not sent to the runtime: it is looked up in the approved plan by step id and passed to the agent as --model. Pause interrupts the running step; the screen says so. Still unseen by MarkView: a question or permission prompt printed in a step agent's own terminal (the silence warning is the mitigation), and the Mac sleeping or MarkView quitting pauses a run.
