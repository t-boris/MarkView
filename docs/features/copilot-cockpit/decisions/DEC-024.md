## Context

A step may be interrupted (MarkView closed, agent session ended, crash) after partially changing files, builds or deployments, so its outcome and side effects are unknown.

## Alternatives

1. Repeat the whole step after warning about possible repeated actions.

## Decision

When a step is interrupted with unknown partial effects, do not repeat or continue it automatically. Show the step's known results and evidence, state that its effects may be incomplete, and let the user choose how to proceed.

## Reason

The interrupted step's state is unknown; automatic repetition could duplicate actions such as file changes or deployments.

## Consequences

Resuming an interrupted run always requires a user decision; the cockpit must capture enough per-step evidence to show what is known about the interrupted step.
