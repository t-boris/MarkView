## Statement

The system shall let the running agent check its own state between chunks, ask the person a question when something is unclear, wait for the answer without losing its place, and report progress.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Between chunks the agent compares its progress with the plan and the requirements and reports what is done, what is next and what it found
- [ ] A question stops the job at a known chunk, is shown with options in the thread, and the job continues from that chunk after the answer
- [ ] A question is also recorded as a question of the feature, with the answer saved as the person's
- [ ] The person is told when the job needs them, or finishes, while the app is in the background
- [ ] The job never waits silently longer than a set time without showing why
