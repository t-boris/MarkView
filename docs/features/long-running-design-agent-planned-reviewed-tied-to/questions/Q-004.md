## Question

Who approves the plan after its review: always the person, or the agent continues alone when the review finds no problems?

## Why it matters

A long job can run for a long time; the plan is the last cheap place to stop a wrong direction.

## Options

- **A**: Always the person approves the plan (default), and the person can switch a job to unattended after approving it
- **B**: The agent continues alone when the review has no blocking finding

## Recommended

A
