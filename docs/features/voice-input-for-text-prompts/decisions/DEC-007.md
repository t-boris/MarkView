## Context

Behavior on close, focus change, second field, key removal, concurrent edits and cancel was undefined.

## Alternatives

1. Stop and discard on close
2. Single active recording
3. Insert at cursor at stop time
4. Insert at cursor at start time

## Decision

Only one recording app-wide. Closing/cancelling the intake sheet or removing the key stops recording/transcription and discards audio with no insertion. Esc or starting the mic on another field cancels the current recording without inserting. Focus change does not stop recording. The transcript is inserted into the originating field at its current cursor position when transcription completes, or appended at the end if that field is not focused; the field stays editable during transcription.

## Reason

Predictable, avoids lost or misplaced text and matches a simple toggle model.

## Consequences

REQ-003 gains acceptance criteria for cancel, close, single-recording and insertion position.
