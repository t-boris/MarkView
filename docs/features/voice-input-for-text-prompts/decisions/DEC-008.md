## Context

Whisper rejects uploads over 25 MB; no cap was specified.

## Alternatives

1. Auto-stop at cap
2. Chunk long audio
3. Error after upload

## Decision

Reuse the Terminal dictation limit if one exists; otherwise cap recording at 10 minutes, show elapsed time, warn 30 s before the cap, auto-stop at the cap and transcribe what was recorded. No chunking.

## Reason

Keeps compressed audio well below 25 MB and bounds cost while still allowing long dictation.

## Consequences

The Terminal limit must be checked during implementation; REQ-003 gains a cap criterion.
