## Context

Mean is outlier-sensitive and uncorrectable errors are likely; time basis and display format were undefined.

## Alternatives

1. Mean
2. Median
3. Median plus mean
4. Working-time durations

## Decision

The per-project summary shows median as the primary statistic plus mean and sample size (feature count) per step and per model. Durations are calendar (wall-clock) time. Durations display as days/hours/minutes (e.g. '2d 3h 15m'), rounded to the minute, omitting leading zero units.

## Reason

Median is robust to mistaken marks; showing mean alongside reveals skew; calendar time needs no work-schedule configuration.

## Consequences

REQ-004 wording changes from 'average' to median+mean; overnight/weekend gaps are included in durations.
