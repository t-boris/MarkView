## Finding

The ARM CI job timed out after its 600-second suite limit. Its test began at 12:49:37 UTC, roughly seven minutes after the job's test step began, and had not finished when the runner killed it at 12:52:19 UTC. The same test passed locally in 79.1 seconds (`/tmp/markview-gui-final-candidate.log`). The CI log includes a system spell-server timeout during the test, but the log does not establish whether that caused the slowdown. Evidence: `/tmp/markview-ci-theme-failure.log` and [CI run 44302387150](https://github.com/t-boris/MarkView/actions/runs/44302387150).

## Resolution

Open. Verify whether earlier reuse of the hosted build cache removes the cold-build delay, investigate the test's CI execution time, and rerun the full test without reducing its checks. DEC-019's end-to-end eight-minute limit remains open.
