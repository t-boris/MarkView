## Finding

The ARM CI job timed out after its 600-second suite limit. Its test began at 12:49:37 UTC, roughly seven minutes after the job's test step began, and had not finished when the runner killed it at 12:52:19 UTC. The same test passed locally in 79.1 seconds (`/tmp/markview-gui-final-candidate.log`). The CI log includes a system spell-server timeout during the test, but the log does not establish whether that caused the slowdown. Evidence: `/tmp/markview-ci-theme-failure.log` and [CI run 44302387150](https://github.com/t-boris/MarkView/actions/runs/44302387150).

## Resolution

The theme fixture now uses the shipping `QAWebWindow` behind other windows and outside all displays, asserting that the pages are visible to WebKit while remaining off-screen and without focus. This targets the hidden-WebKit clock behavior seen in F-007 without removing checks. The effect on the theme test and its CI duration remains unverified; the system spell-server message has not been established as the cause. DEC-019's end-to-end eight-minute limit remains open.

The first hosted build exposed an initializer error: an assertion closure captured the window before `super.init()`. The window ordering and assertions were moved after superclass initialization. The complete theme test then passed locally with the ARM hosted configuration in 79.1 seconds (`/tmp/markview-theme-qawindow-arm.log`), including the page-visibility and no-focus assertions. CI timing remains unverified.
