## Statement

The system shall provide shared infrastructure in Packages/MarkViewCore linked directly into the MarkView target (no duplicate MarkView/Core copy), with FU-04 helper defects fixed and initial production callers wired (the call-site migrations of AR-18, AR-25 and the Swift half of AR-27 are done by count on `feat/architecture-review-finish`; AR-24 and the web half of AR-27 are partial).
