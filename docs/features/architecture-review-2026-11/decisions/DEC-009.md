## Context

Section 9 (AR-40) and Section 13 #9 asked whether per-window tokens and native confirmation for by_user steps on the control socket are required. During follow-up (`FU-03`), the owner clarified the authentication model for the control socket.

## Alternatives

1. Keep unauthenticated uid-only control socket
2. Enforce both per-instance tokens and a separate socket-level `by_user` confirmation gate

## Decision

Require per-instance/per-window cryptographic tokens (mode `0600` next to the socket) plus kernel peer UID/PID checks on the control socket, with fail-closed constant-time verification and per-socket client lookup, without a separate socket-level `by_user` confirmation gate (see `DEC-011`).

## Reason

Prevents untrusted local processes or child processes (no `setenv` export into the app environment) from driving MarkView while keeping terminal/automation workflows ergonomic without redundant socket-level prompts.

## Consequences

Control socket connections require a per-instance token (mode `0600`, read per socket path, cleaned up on quit, verified fail-closed in constant time against kernel peer UID == `getuid()`). The unused `byUserConfirmationGate` claim is removed.
