## Context

Section 9 (AR-40) and Section 13 #9 ask whether per-window tokens and native confirmation for by_user steps on the control socket are acceptable.

## Alternatives

1. Keep unauthenticated uid-only control socket; use per-window tokens without native confirmation for by_user

## Decision

Require per-window/per-instance tokens on the control socket and native confirmation for by_user mutations.

## Reason

Owner decision #9: prevents untrusted local processes from driving MarkView or spoofing by_user approval.

## Consequences

Control socket connections require a per-instance/per-window token (written to a mode-0600 file and passed to spawned terminals/MCP processes) and mutations claiming by_user: true require native confirmation unless explicitly pre-authorized in an interactive prompt.
