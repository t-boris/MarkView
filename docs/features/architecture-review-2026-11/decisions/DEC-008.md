## Context

Section 11 (AR-45) and Section 13 #8 ask whether to consolidate CI into one workflow with caching and concurrency cancellation.

## Alternatives

1. Keep 14 separate workflows without concurrency cancellation; add concurrency only without consolidating workflows

## Decision

Consolidate CI checks into ci.yml with build caching and concurrency (cancel-in-progress: true).

## Reason

Owner decision #8: eliminates redundant cold builds and cancels superseded runs on new pushes.

## Consequences

Consolidate repetitive PR/push CI checks into a unified ci.yml with build caching, manifest-driven suite execution, and concurrency cancel-in-progress: true.
