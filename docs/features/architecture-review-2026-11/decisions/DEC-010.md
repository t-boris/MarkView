## Context

Section 10 (AR-43), Section 11 (AR-47), and Section 13 #10 ask whether to adopt targeted strict concurrency checking and a formatter per package.

## Alternatives

1. Leave SWIFT_STRICT_CONCURRENCY unset and skip formatting configuration

## Decision

Adopt targeted strict concurrency checking and formatting configuration for packages and targets.

## Reason

Owner decision #10: catches Sendable and actor-isolation bugs at compile time and standardises formatting.

## Consequences

Enable targeted strict concurrency checking and add formatting/editor configuration (.editorconfig / swift-format configuration) as packages and targets are updated.
