## Context

The owner selected option A in Q-004. AI responses and settings were already mandatory; the remaining coverage was undecided.

## Alternatives

1. A. Apply the setting to the entire interface, including the editor, AI responses, settings, terminal, sidebars, labels, and buttons.
2. B. Apply it only to the editor, AI responses, and settings.
3. C. Apply it to AI responses and settings, with other areas selected separately.

## Decision

Apply the application font size setting to text throughout the interface, including the editor, AI responses, settings, terminal, sidebars, labels, and buttons.

## Reason

The owner explicitly chose consistent coverage across the entire interface.

## Consequences

The selected size applies across the entire interface. Existing text wrapping or scrolling behavior must preserve readability when space is limited.
