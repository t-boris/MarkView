## Context

Q-002 distinguishes displaying scanned pages from recognizing their text.

## Alternatives

1. Offer on-demand OCR for scanned documents.

## Decision

Display scanned pages; search, selection, and copying use existing text layers only.

## Reason

The stakeholder explicitly selected rendering without OCR.

## Consequences

Image-only scans remain readable but cannot be searched or copied as recognized text.
