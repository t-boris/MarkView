## Context

The first release needed a defined interpretation of full PDF reading support.

## Alternatives

1. A: Essential reading controls only.
2. C: Complete reading plus saved highlights and notes.

## Decision

Include essential reading controls, thumbnails, document outlines when present, in-document text search, text selection and copying, and internal and external links. Saved highlights and notes are outside this release's reader scope.

## Reason

The stakeholder selected option B, establishing comprehensive reading without markup capabilities.

## Consequences

Navigation, text interaction, and links are required. Reader OCR and new PDF workspace-search or AI integration are excluded from this release. Existing PDF feature-source ingestion remains unchanged (Q-002, Q-003, DEC-002, DEC-003).
