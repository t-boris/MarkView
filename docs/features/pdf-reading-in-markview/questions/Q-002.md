## Question

Should scanned PDFs only display their pages, or also become searchable and selectable through OCR?

## Why it matters

Displaying scanned pages and recognizing their text are different product capabilities; existing extraction only reads embedded PDF text.

## Options

- **A**: Display scanned pages; search and selection use existing text layers only.
- **B**: Offer on-demand OCR for scanned documents.

## Recommended

A — Rendering all pages satisfies reading support; OCR is a separate capability that should not be assumed.

## Answer

A. Display scanned pages; search and selection use existing text layers only.
