## Question

Beyond reading, should PDFs participate in workspace search and AI workflows in this release?

## Why it matters

Existing PDF ingestion for feature sources does not establish general PDF search or AI behavior.

## Options

- **A**: Reader only; preserve existing PDF source-ingestion behavior.
- **B**: Also include PDF text in workspace search, with results opening the matching page.
- **C**: Include B and let users ask AI about an open PDF with page references.

## Answer
