## Question

Which reader capabilities must the first release include?

## Why it matters

"Full PDF support" does not establish a testable reader feature set.

## Options

- **A**: Essential reader: scrolling, zoom, fit-to-page/width, page count, and jump to page.
- **B**: Complete read-only reader: A plus thumbnails, document outline when present, in-document text search, text selection/copy, and internal/external links.
- **C**: Reader and markup: B plus saved highlights and notes.

## Recommended

B — It delivers comprehensive reading without expanding the request into PDF authoring.

## Answer

B. Complete read-only reader: A plus thumbnails, document outline when present, in-document text search, text selection/copy, and internal/external links.
