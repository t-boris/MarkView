## Finding

The accepted decision covers every supported agent, but the requirement limits third-party services to Copilot and the no-connection guarantee to Copilot. Implementers cannot tell whether other agents must also expose and disable their third-party services, including in their own configurations.

> Все поддерживаемые агенты, с отдельным выбором для каждого.

## Possible interpretations

- Manage every available MCP service for every supported agent, with the same launch-context behavior.
- Manage all Copilot services but only MarkView services for other agents.

## Resolution

Для каждого поддерживаемого агента управлять всеми его MCP-сервисами, включая сторонние, и предотвращать прямое подключение к отключённым сервисам. — see DEC-003.
