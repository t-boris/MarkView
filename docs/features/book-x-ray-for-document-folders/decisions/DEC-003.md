## Context

Arrows between sections can come from the links the authors wrote, from the AI, or both.

## Alternatives

1. A: Markdown links with anchors, wiki-links and reference links resolved to the section, plus AI-found related sections marked as inferred.
2. B: Explicit links only.

## Decision

A. Explicit links are solid `links` edges; AI-inferred ones are dashed `related` edges with the reason in the panel.

## Reason

Documents without links would otherwise show no connections; marking the AI's links keeps them distinguishable from the authors' own.
