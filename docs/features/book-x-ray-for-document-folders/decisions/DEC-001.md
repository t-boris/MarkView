## Context

A book needs parts, chapters and sections. They can follow the folders, files and headings, or the AI can compose them.

## Alternatives

1. A: Folders → parts, documents → chapters, headings H1–H6 → sections; the AI writes names, annotations and related links.
2. B: The AI groups documents into thematic chapters and orders them; sections stay headings.
3. C: The AI may also split and merge sections across documents.

## Decision

A. The structure is deterministic and always matches the files; the AI only annotates.

## Reason

Cheapest and predictable; every node maps to a file and a line, so opening a section always works; the AI's part can be redone without changing the map.
