## Context

Section annotations are what makes the book readable at a glance; they cost one AI call per document.

## Alternatives

1. A: One call per document during Analyze, cached by the document's content signature, output language and prompt version; a repeated Analyze redoes only changed documents.
2. B: On demand, when a chapter is expanded or a button is pressed.

## Decision

A, with a cap of 150 calls per Analyze and "Describe remaining chapters" / "Describe this chapter" for the rest and for single chapters.

## Reason

Seeing everything at once is the point; caching keeps repeats free; the cap bounds the first run on big folders.
