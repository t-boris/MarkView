## Context

The explanatory answer must be the primary result of I Need to Understand, not an unnoticed line in the X-Ray details panel.

## Alternatives

1. A: Separate window/sheet with the answer and 'show in X-Ray' links
2. B: X-Ray opens with the answer prominently expanded in the right panel
3. C: Answer opens as a draft RES-nnn document in the editor

## Decision

B: X-Ray opens as today, and the answer is shown prominently expanded in the right (details) panel alongside the highlighted places.

## Reason

Answer and evidence are visible together; smallest change to the existing flow.

## Consequences

Long answers must stay readable in the side panel (scrolling, sections). Saving remains a separate action (REQ-004); no new window.
