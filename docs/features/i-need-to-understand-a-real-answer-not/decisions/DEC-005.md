## Context

REQ-004 says the answer can be saved; when and how was open.

## Alternatives

1. Auto-save every answer as RES
2. No saving

## Decision

Provide a 'Save as research' action on the answer that creates a new RES-nnn document with question, answer and sources; no auto-save.

## Reason

Auto-save would clutter docs/research with throwaway questions.

## Consequences

Unsaved answers are lost when X-Ray filter is closed.
