## Statement

Submitting an I Need to Understand question (from the intake sheet or from a document) shall open X-Ray with the written answer expanded at the top of the right panel as the main, immediately visible output; X-Ray highlights are supporting evidence.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] After submission the answer is visible without any extra click or scroll
- [x] The answer is above highlight/details content in the right panel
- [x] Both entry points (intake sheet, from document) produce the same result

Implementation and verification: [2.23.0 verification](../implementation/verification.md).
