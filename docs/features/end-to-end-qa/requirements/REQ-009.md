## Statement

The system shall record a video of a running scenario by default, let the person switch the recording off, and show subtitles along the bottom of the video that say what is being done at that moment, where (the control clicked or the field filled) and why.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Video recording is on by default; the person can switch it off before a run, and a run with recording off records nothing and says so in its report.
- [ ] While recording, a band of subtitles at the bottom of the video states, for the action in progress, what is done, where (the control or field, named as a person would name it) and why (the purpose within the step and the scenario).
- [ ] A subtitle appears when its action begins and stays until the next action begins; a check is subtitled with what is being verified and then with the result.
- [ ] The subtitle band is added below the picture, so it never covers the application.
- [ ] In a run with several players, a subtitle names the player whose turn it is.
- [ ] Subtitles follow the language of the scenario and are written from the runner's own record of the action and the AI's one-line explanation of it.
- [ ] Subtitles pass the same masking step as the frames: a value typed into a hidden field, and any text the policy hides, never appears in them; a masked field is named by its label.
- [ ] The same lines appear in the live feed and in the run report.
