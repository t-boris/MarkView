## Statement

The scenario graph shall zoom around the cursor and move by dragging, as the other canvases of MarkView do.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The mouse wheel and a pinch zoom around the cursor between 10% and 250%
- [ ] Shift with the wheel, and dragging the empty background, move the graph
- [ ] A drag that starts on a step still connects it to another step and does not move the graph
- [ ] The percentage button fits the whole graph; another scenario opens fitted
