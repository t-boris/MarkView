## Context

Control over QA execution

## Alternatives

1. Require approval of preparation and the scenario, with explicit confirmation of production data changes.
2. Execute preparation and actions automatically after scenario approval in every environment.

## Decision

Require user approval of preparation and the scenario before execution, and explicit confirmation for production actions that change data.

## Reason

The product owner selected explicit control over preparation and production changes.

## Consequences

Preparation cannot begin before approval, and production data-changing actions require explicit confirmation.
