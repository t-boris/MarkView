## Context

DEC-004 and DEC-016 had the person choose which pages and data may be saved and sent, and had everything saved or sent pass one masking step that failed closed. Boris, the only person who uses MarkView, said on 2026-11-09: "давай без маскировки … никому это не надо, кроме меня", and that he wants the feature as soon as possible.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the page policy and the masking as designed.
2. Keep only the list of pages that may be recorded.

## Decision

The feature has no masking step and no policy of allowed pages. Frames, pictures, subtitles and the page text and images the assistant reads are kept and sent as they are. Two plain rules stay, because they cost nothing: the runner never reads the value of a password field into what it sends to the assistant, and a saved login's password stays in the Keychain and is typed by the runner itself, never shown to the assistant. Runs stay in `.dde/qa/`, which carries its own `.gitignore`.

## Reason

It is the owner's call, the only user needs none of it, and the step would delay everything else. Removing it also removes a gate that stopped a run until pages were allowed.

## Consequences

DEC-004 and DEC-016 no longer apply. The material-policy stage (S4) is dropped from the plan. Requirements REQ-004, REQ-005, REQ-008 and REQ-009 lose their criteria about choosing pages and hiding data. If MarkView is ever used by other people, a policy and masking would have to be designed again.
