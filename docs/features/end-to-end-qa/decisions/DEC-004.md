## Context

Sensitive data in recordings and AI analysis

## Alternatives

1. Let the user specify what may be saved and transmitted, and hide sensitive data.
2. Enable recording and analysis automatically while hiding known secrets.

## Decision

Before recording and AI analysis, let the user specify which pages and data may be saved and transmitted, and hide sensitive data.

## Reason

The product owner selected user control over recording and transmission.

## Consequences

Recording and AI analysis must respect the user's choices about pages and data.
