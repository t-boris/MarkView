## Statement

The system shall let a process say who may confirm its delivery event, a person only (the default) or a person or an AI with evidence, and shall let an assistant confirm the event through MCP when the process allows it, so that MarkView then completes the work like it does after a person's confirmation.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The Processes page has 'Who confirms' for a process whose delivery event is confirmed by someone: 'A person only' or 'A person, or an AI with evidence'; the Done panel sentence, the process summary and the diagram card say which
- [ ] A workflow file without the setting reads as 'A person only', and an app that does not know the setting still reads the file (the field is optional, schema stays 1)
- [ ] markview_confirm_delivery(issue, evidence) records the confirmation with the AI as actor and the evidence text, and the Lifecycle history shows who confirmed and what was verified
- [ ] It is refused with the reason when the process says a person only, when the process has no confirmed event, when the item is not under way or completed, and when the evidence text is empty
- [ ] After an AI's confirmation MarkView completes the item once the rest of the delivery evidence is there (merged, checks, release), exactly as after a person's
- [ ] A person's confirmation in the window works as before, and the AI's confirmation does not count for a process that says a person only, even if a record was written by hand
