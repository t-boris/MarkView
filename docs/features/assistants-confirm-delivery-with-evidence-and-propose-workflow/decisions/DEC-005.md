## Context

A proposal is a draft that the person is meant to look at at once, in the conversation where he asked for it.

## Alternatives

1. Store proposals in the project (.dde/workflow/proposals), which would carry a second copy of the rules and old proposals to clean up

## Decision

A window keeps at most one workflow proposal, in memory, with who proposed it, when, the summary, the revision it was made on and the workflow. It is not written to disk and is gone when the window closes or the app restarts; the assistant proposes again.

## Reason

Smallest thing that does the job; nothing to migrate or clean up, and nothing on disk that looks like the project's workflow.

## Consequences

Closing the window loses an unreviewed proposal.
