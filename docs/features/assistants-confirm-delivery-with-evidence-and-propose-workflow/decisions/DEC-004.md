## Context

Two bugs stayed in review although the assistant had restarted and checked the installed app, because the event 'Installed on mac' could only be confirmed by a person.

## Alternatives

1. Let every assistant confirm every event (breaks processes that need a person to say a thing was deployed)
2. A new delivery event type 'ai-verified' instead of a setting (a second place that duplicates the event name and loses the existing human-confirmed files)

## Decision

A process with a confirmed delivery event has an optional setting for who confirms it: a person only (the default, and the reading of every existing file) or a person or an AI with evidence, the same two choices that acceptance has. An AI confirms with markview_confirm_delivery and an evidence text of what it verified and how.

## Reason

Boris: the assistant verified the new version, so what more evidence is needed. Acceptance already allows 'a person, or an AI with evidence'; the delivery event gets the same choice, defaulting to the safe one.

## Consequences

The stored process gains one optional field. A person can still confirm in the window. An assistant cannot confirm for a process that says a person only.
