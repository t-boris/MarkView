## Statement

The system shall title each lane of the transition diagram by what its states mean to a person (Backlog and specification or To do, In progress, Done, Cancelled) without changing any stored role or status.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A not-started lane whose states are joined by specification steps is titled 'Backlog & specification'; any other is titled 'To do'
- [ ] The active lane is titled 'In progress' and the completed lane 'Done'
- [ ] The step kind formerly named 'Specification' is shown as 'Stage change', because it also joins stages of a flow that has no specification
- [ ] No role, status id or file content changes
