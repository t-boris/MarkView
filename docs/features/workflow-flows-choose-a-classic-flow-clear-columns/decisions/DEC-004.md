## Context



## Alternatives

1. Treat every feature of a board as still in Explore
2. Treat every feature of a board as past Explore

## Decision

With the stages (a flow that has "exploring") a feature is past Explore when its status is after Draft, as before. In a flow without them it is past Explore once work has started or finished (the role of its status is not waiting) or every active requirement is approved. New features start in the preferred stage when the flow has it, else in the first waiting state of the flow.

## Reason

The status of a Kanban item (Backlog, Ready) cannot tell whether discovery is done. Without this rule every feature of a board would count as past Explore, the AI would write its requirements as approved at once and the guided flow would refuse Explore. It follows from DEC-001: Explore and Review run in every flow and leave the status as it is.

## Consequences

A feature on a board that is in Ready with draft requirements can still be explored; after Finish Explore approves the requirements the guided steps Ready, Plan and Implement open.
