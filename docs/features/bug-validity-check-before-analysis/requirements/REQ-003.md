## Statement

Stop investigation of the resolved problem after automatic local closure has been saved successfully.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] After successful automatic closure, the invocation performs no further investigation of the resolved problem and generates no further clarification questions about it.
- [ ] If saving closure fails, the result reports the failure and does not claim that the bug was closed.
