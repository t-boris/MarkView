## Context

Finding F-002 identified a gap in REQ-009. The requirement records unavailable measurements but leaves eligibility for default adoption undefined when baseline and candidate costs cannot be compared. The gate applies to all proposed defaults, including deep Research.

## Alternatives

1. Record the cost evidence gap and require a material quality gain only when the comparison shows an increase in cost or duration.
2. Defer making the candidate the default until comparable cost data are available, even if a material quality gain has already been demonstrated.

## Decision

When comparable cost data are unavailable, require a demonstrated material quality gain unless proxy evidence agreed in advance establishes that cost did not increase. Any measured increase in duration or cost requires a demonstrated material quality gain.

## Reason

This rule accounts for a possible cost increase when direct measurement is unavailable. It allows a candidate to qualify through a demonstrated material quality gain or credible evidence that cost did not increase, while preserving the quality requirement for measured regressions.

## Consequences

REQ-009 must record the cost evidence gap, any proxy evidence agreed in advance and what it establishes, and the quality result against the predefined criteria. If deep Research fails the gate, revise it and repeat the comparison before releasing the feature.
