## Statement

The system shall show each group on the plan screen as steps that run together, and let the person take steps out of it before approving.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Members of a group are shown together and labelled as running at the same time, in the plan screen and in the approval summary.
- [ ] The person can take one step out of a group; a group left with one member is no longer a group.
- [ ] Moving, duplicating or deleting a member keeps the remaining group valid (adjacent, 2 to 3 members) or removes the label.
- [ ] "Start from" offers only a group's first member, never a later member.
- [ ] Any change to a group changes the plan's fingerprint, so the plan must be approved again.
