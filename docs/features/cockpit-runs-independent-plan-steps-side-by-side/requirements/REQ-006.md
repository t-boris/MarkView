## Statement

The system shall apply the run's limits to a group and recover a group after an interruption or an app restart.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The run's active time grows by the group's wall-clock duration, not by the sum of its members (DEC-003).
- [ ] Each member keeps its own step clock, fix-cycle meter and limit watch.
- [ ] After an interruption or an app restart every member that was running is marked interrupted; Continue runs the group's unfinished members again and keeps the passed ones.
- [ ] A run saved with groups can still be opened by an app build without this feature (its current step is the group's first member).
