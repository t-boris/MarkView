## Question

Should running steps together have its own switch in the plan's guardrails?

## Why it matters

A switch changes the planner prompt, the controls (fingerprint) and the approval bar; without one the only way to opt out is editing each group.

## Options

- **A**: A "Parallel steps" switch, on in every new plan like delegation (DEC-038 of agent-aware-implementation-runs); off removes the groups
- **B**: No switch: the planner's groups are always kept; the person ungroups steps in the editor

## Recommended

A

## Answer

A: a "Parallel steps" switch, on in every new plan like delegation (DEC-038); off removes the groups.
