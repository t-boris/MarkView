## Question

Should a managed (feature) run's review be split into reviews by two agents that run together, in this feature?

## Why it matters

Managed steps are all prerequisites with fixed order and findings rules (DEC-030); grouping them changes delivery, not only scheduling.

## Options

- **A**: Not now: managed delivery steps stay as they are (they are prerequisites); a later feature
- **B**: Yes: the review becomes a group of two reviews by different agents

## Recommended

A

## Answer

B: yes, in this feature: a managed run's review becomes a group of two reviews by different agents that run together.
