## Context

The run's time limit adds up step durations (activeSeconds); with members running together a sum would count the same minutes two or three times.

## Alternatives

1. Sum the members' durations (agent time)

## Decision

A group adds its wall-clock duration (from its first start to its last end) to the run's active time; each member keeps its own step clock for display.

## Reason

The time limit protects the person's waiting time, which a group shortens; the cost of the members shows in their own meters.

## Consequences

A run with groups may spend more agent time than its time limit shows.
