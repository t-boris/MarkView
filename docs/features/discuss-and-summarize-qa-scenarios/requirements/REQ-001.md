## Statement

The system shall ask focused AI clarification questions before generating a new QA scenario and include the person's answers in generation.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] The New scenario flow offers AI questions with answer choices and editable free-text answers.
- [x] Questions address missing intent, participants, initial state, success checks and overlooked failure cases without repeating settled facts.
- [x] The person may ask another round or proceed to generation, and answers reach the scenario generator.
- [x] Cancellation and assistant failure preserve the entered idea, attachments and answers.
