## Finding

The prototype brief describes the central map opening and nodes appearing progressively as research develops. The requirement specifies the structure of the completed result but not whether progress is visible in the map or whether the map opens only after generation. These are materially different research experiences.

> Initial AI research identifies a central subject, develops main branches, and adds progressively finer topics.

## Possible interpretations

- Open the map at the start and progressively show researched branches.
- Run research in the background and open the completed or interrupted map afterward.

## Resolution

Открывать вкладку карты в начале исследования и постепенно показывать готовые узлы и ветви. — see DEC-004.
