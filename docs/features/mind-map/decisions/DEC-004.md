## Context

Initial research builds a map from a central subject into progressively finer topics. The approved requirements do not specify when the map opens or how progress appears.

## Alternatives

1. Run initial research in the background and open the completed or interrupted map afterward.
2. Open the map immediately and show research status, but populate it only after completion or interruption.

## Decision

Open the research map in its own tab when initial research starts, and progressively display ready nodes and branches as research proceeds.

## Reason

The team chose to make the evolving research visible in the map during initial generation.

## Consequences

Update REQ-002 to require opening the map tab at the start and progressively displaying ready nodes and branches. The behavior when research is stopped or fails still needs to be specified, including how partial results are retained and identified.
