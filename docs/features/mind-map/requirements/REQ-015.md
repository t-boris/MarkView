## Statement

The system shall let the person change the parent of a node by dragging from a small arrow on another node, and shall let the person delete a logical connection by clicking its line.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Every node shows a small arrow outside its outer edge (right edge; left edge for a node in a branch that grows to the left) on hover and when selected; the arrow does not collide with the resize handles, the fold button or the node menu
- [ ] Dragging from the arrow of node A draws a line that follows the pointer; nodes that may become A's child are highlighted under the pointer and the others show that they cannot; releasing on empty canvas or pressing Esc changes nothing
- [ ] Releasing on node B makes B the last child of A, with its whole subtree, title, properties, evidence and logical connections; the layout places the moved branch under A and the hand-placed positions of the moved nodes are dropped
- [ ] A move that would put a node under itself or under one of its descendants, a move of the root, and a move under the node that is already the parent are refused in code and the page says why; the file stays as it was
- [ ] The file is rewritten through the single writer, the outline and the node ids stay valid, and the change shows in the Source and View modes
- [ ] Clicking a dashed connection line selects it and shows a small cross in the middle of the line; clicking the cross or pressing Delete or Backspace removes that one connection; the line is easy to hit (a wider invisible hit area) and the cross in the properties panel still works
- [ ] Clicking a hierarchy line deletes nothing
- [ ] The arrow, the drop highlight, the selected line and the cross follow the app theme in dark and light and keep their size at any zoom; plain wheel zoom and canvas dragging are unchanged
