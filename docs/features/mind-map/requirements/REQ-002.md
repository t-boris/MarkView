## Statement

Generate an initial research map inside-out, from the central subject to progressively finer topics, opening it in its own tab when research starts and progressively displaying ready nodes and branches, while preserving the existing research workflow's traceability and incomplete-result handling in an editable Markdown file.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Initial AI research identifies a central subject, develops main branches, and adds progressively finer topics.
- [ ] The research map opens in its own tab when initial research starts.
- [ ] Ready nodes and branches are progressively displayed in the map as research proceeds.
- [ ] The map retains accessible sources, distinguishes project facts, external facts, AI inferences, and open assumptions, and records web queries in its editable Markdown file.
- [ ] Existing research inputs and the repository's web-search setting apply to initial research.
- [ ] An interrupted or incomplete research result is identified as incomplete and can be continued.
