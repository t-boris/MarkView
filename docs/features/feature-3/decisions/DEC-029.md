## Context



## Alternatives

1. Block all non-specification transitions of an item until a person chooses its variant.
2. Record a replacement as a forced transition (it would turn verified completion into an override).

## Decision

A work item created without a person's choice (by an AI tool, by the intake or by a basket) gets the project's default variant for its type, marked as the default; the app's own creation and detail views let a person choose another variant at any time, and every assignment is recorded with who and when. Deleting a status or a variant that work items use needs a person's replacement; the affected items are moved by a record of its own kind (replaced) that keeps the earlier history and does not count as an override or as delivery evidence. A replaced completed status keeps its completion state only when the replacement is also a completed or cancelled one.

## Reason

DEC-019 asks a person to choose; a tool-created item has no person at hand, and blocking tools until someone chooses would stop agents. A default that is visible and changeable keeps the choice the person's. A separate record kind keeps verified completion from being faked or lost when the workflow changes.

## Consequences

The project default per type is a setting of the workflow. The history shows the variant in force for each transition.
